Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Articles are selected because they address a practical workflow-design question. We aim for clear scope, original explanation, and links that help readers navigate the site—not pages built only to capture keyword variations.

Corrections and updates

Material factual errors should be corrected when verified. Articles are dated so readers can judge their currency. We do not fabricate testing, credentials, reviews, endorsements, or data.

Commercial independence

The site does not publish paid placements or product endorsements. If that changes, the disclosure will be made clearly on the affected page.
