About Workflow Signal

Last updated: September 2026

Workflow Signal publishes practical, vendor-neutral explainers about AI-assisted workflow design. The editorial aim is to help readers ask better questions about process ownership, review, evidence and risk. We do not sell software, accept paid placements, or claim that automation is appropriate for every task.

Our approach

We prefer specific, bounded guidance over promises. When a subject needs legal, security, financial, medical or specialist advice, readers should consult an appropriately qualified professional for their situation.
